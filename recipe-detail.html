Paneer Butter Masala

The perfect restaurant-style creamy, tangy, and rich North Indian curry.

Cuisine: North Indian
Prep Time: 20 Mins
Cook Time: 30 Mins
Servings: 4 People

Ingredients

  • 250g Paneer (Cottage Cheese), cubed
  • 4 large Tomatoes, roughly chopped
  • 1 large Onion, roughly chopped
  • 1 inch Ginger
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 1/4 cup Cashews
  • 2 tbsp Butter (unsalted)
  • 1 tbsp Oil
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri Red Chili Powder
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 1 tsp Garam Masala
  • 1 tsp Kasuri Methi (Dried Fenugreek)
  • 1/4 cup Fresh Cream
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Prepare the Gravy Base: Heat oil and 1 tbsp butter in a pan. Add onion, ginger, garlic, and cashews. Sauté for 2-3 minutes. Add chopped tomatoes and sauté until soft and mushy.
  2. Blend: Cool the mixture completely and blend into a smooth paste. Add a little water if needed.
  3. Cook the Gravy: Strain the paste into the same pan for a silky smooth gravy. Add the remaining butter. Add chili powder, turmeric, and salt. Cook for 5-7 minutes until the gravy thickens and oil separates.
  4. Add Paneer & Finish: Add the paneer cubes, garam masala, and crushed kasuri methi. Simmer for 5 minutes. Stir in the fresh cream and turn off the heat. Serve hot with naan or rice.
